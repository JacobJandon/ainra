The oath: this explorer renders public artifacts, verifies them in your browser, and orders everything mechanically. It issues nothing, revokes nothing, ranks nothing, and shows no green it did not compute. If this page ever disagrees with the cryptographic record — the record wins, and that is a bug.

Colophon & independence. AINRAscan is separate from the AINRA root, which is constitutionally barred from operating an explorer: a registry that controls how it is seen is a registry you must trust. This app runs no part of the registry and reads only public artifacts — a root-accredited registrar directory, log checkpoints, inclusion proofs, and signed status lists — which it re-derives with the open @ainra/sdk and CC0 conformance vectors. It carries zero telemetry: nothing you do here is measured or sent anywhere. This deployment renders the staging network on a TEST-ROOT; every artifact and verdict is real cryptography over clearly-labeled placeholder operators, never production and never a claim of usage.
