# The fourteen days

The machine is public, live, self-verifying and finished. Three rows in [`docs/DOD.md`](../docs/DOD.md) are still
open, and no amount of engineering moves them: **≥3 independent verifiers**, **a recorded custodian ceremony**, and
**a 14-day soak**. Each one begins with a person deciding to spend an afternoon on us. This is the schedule for
asking them.

One primary action per day, done before noon. Run `make campaign-status` each morning — it tells you which day it
is, what today's action is, and what the registries actually say.

The nudge rhythm is deliberately not a calendar day. It is mechanical — **three days after a send, exactly one
nudge, one sentence, then stop** — so `make campaign-status` surfaces it every morning instead of batching it.

## The K1 math, honestly

Eight completed interviews by D14 needs roughly ten booked, which needs **25–30 asks out by D5** at ordinary
cold-outreach reply rates. The calendar delivers that only if D2 actually happens: ten interview asks on D2, five on
D3, five on D5. Skip D2 and the gate is already lost on D5 — the arithmetic will not forgive it later.

K4 works the same way: three attestations by 05 Sep wants **all twenty asks in flight by D9**, because a verifier
who says yes still needs an afternoon, and the reply-then-run gap is a week in practice.

## The slippage rule

If D9's count says K1 will miss, **re-date it in the open**:

```sh
node tools/campaign.mjs redate K1 2026-08-30 --reason "5 interviews done at D9; the ask volume landed 4 days late"
```

That appends to [`gates.json`](gates.json), regenerates [`GATES.md`](GATES.md), and leaves a permanent public record
of the move. A gate's integrity outranks a gate's date. What is forbidden is quietly reading the gate later, or
counting something looser as an interview because the number was short.

## What this calendar does not do

It does not start the soak. The soak clock begins the day production infrastructure has a reason to exist — genesis
day, per [`docs/genesis-day/RUNBOOK.md`](../docs/genesis-day/RUNBOOK.md) — and starting it early to make a row go
green would be exactly the kind of self-issued evidence this whole project exists to refuse.

It also does not move any Definition-of-Done row. No command in `tools/campaign.mjs` can. Rows move when a stranger's
signed artifact survives verification, and never before.

---

**A note on the dates.** This calendar was written from a pack whose weekday labels were one day off the real 2026
calendar (03 Aug 2026 is a Monday, not a Sunday). The fourteen actions are the pack's, in the pack's order; the days
were re-anchored to the real week so the weekend cluster lands on the actual weekend and the regulator letter lands
on a business day. K1 is read on **D14 = Sun 16 Aug**, a full fourteen days from D1. K4 is unchanged at **05 Sep**.
