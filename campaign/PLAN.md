# The fourteen steps

The machine is public, live, self-verifying and finished. Three rows in [`docs/DOD.md`](../docs/DOD.md) are still
open, and no amount of engineering moves them: **≥3 independent verifiers**, **a recorded custodian ceremony**, and
**a 14-day soak**. Each one begins with a person deciding to spend an afternoon on us. This is the sequence for
asking them.

**A sequence, not a schedule.** There are no dates here. You advance a step when the previous one is actually done,
not when a calendar says so — `make campaign-status` shows which step you are on, what it is, and the command that
performs it. What matters is the *order*, and the order is not a preference: asks have to be out before replies can
arrive, and replies have to arrive before anything can be counted.

The follow-up rhythm is not a step either. It is a **queue**: anyone asked and not yet replied sits in it until they
get their one nudge — one sentence — and then they leave it forever. `make campaign-status` shows the queue every
time you run it, so nobody is forgotten and nobody is chased twice.

## The arithmetic that makes K1 possible

Eight completed interviews needs roughly ten booked, which needs **most of the thirty asks already out** before the
interview steps begin. That is why the asks are front-loaded into steps 2, 3 and 5 and the interviews do not start
until step 6: at ordinary cold-outreach reply rates, an ask sent after the interviews begin is an interview that
happens after the reading. Skipping step 2 does not delay K1 — it decides it.

K4 works the same way. Three attestations wants **all twenty asks in flight by step 9**, because a verifier who says
yes still needs an afternoon, and the gap between "yes" and "done" is where the real waiting happens.

## The honesty rule

When a gate is read, record the reading — whatever it says:

```sh
node tools/campaign.mjs record K1 continuing --reason "5 interviews done; the ask volume went out late"
```

That appends to [`gates.json`](gates.json) with the count at that moment and regenerates [`GATES.md`](GATES.md),
leaving a permanent public record. `--reason` is required and the command refuses without it. What is forbidden is
reading a gate quietly, or counting something looser as an interview because the number was short.

## What this sequence does not do

It does not start the soak. The soak clock begins when production infrastructure has a reason to exist — genesis
day, per [`docs/genesis-day/RUNBOOK.md`](../docs/genesis-day/RUNBOOK.md) — and starting it early to make a row go
green would be exactly the kind of self-issued evidence this whole project exists to refuse.

It also does not move any Definition-of-Done row. No command in `tools/campaign.mjs` can. Rows move when a stranger's
signed artifact survives verification, and never before.
