# DRAFT — FOR LICENSED REVIEW, NOT LEGAL ADVICE

**Nothing in this folder is legal advice, and nothing in it decides anything.** It is a preparation pack for
founding a Bulgarian **сдружение** (non-profit association) that could operate the AINRA root, written so that a
licensed Bulgarian lawyer can review a concrete draft rather than a description of one. The decision on whether to
incorporate at all — and where — remains open in [`../JURISDICTION.md`](../JURISDICTION.md).

## What was verified, and how

Every legal claim in this pack was fetched from a primary source: the consolidated texts of the **ЗЮЛНЦ**, the
**ЗТРРЮЛНЦ**, **Наредба № 1**, the **Закон за счетоводството** and the **ЗННД** on lex.bg, plus the Registry
Agency's own guidance and its euro fee extract. Each claim below carries its article number and a literal
Bulgarian quotation.

Three things the research pass turned up that matter more than the paperwork:

1. **The Registry Agency's own instruction sheet cites the wrong article for who may be a founder.** It attributes
   the rule to **чл. 52**, which is about branches of foreign non-profits. The founders rule is **чл. 5**. Cite
   чл. 5; treat the AV document as guidance, not as law.
2. **The Notary Chamber domain given in most secondary sources is no longer the Notary Chamber.** The `.org`
   domain now serves an unrelated site. The chamber is at **notary-chamber.bg**.
3. **A сдружение almost certainly does NOT need notarised founding documents** — the notarial requirement in
   ЗЮЛНЦ чл. 33, ал. 2 applies to *foundations*, and Наредба № 1 pointedly omits „нотариално заверен" from the
   specimen-signature item for associations where it includes it for commercial forms. A notary is needed in one
   place: filing by a proxy (ЗТРРЮЛНЦ чл. 15, ал. 6).

## The two questions only a lawyer can answer

**(a) Does AINRA's purpose fit the closed list for обществена полза?** Public-benefit designation is
**irrevocable once entered** (чл. 2, ал. 2) and its permitted purposes are a closed statutory list (чл. 38, ал. 1).
A neutral identity root plausibly sits under „развитието и утвърждаването на гражданското общество… и доброто
управление" or under „технологиите" — but *plausibly* is not a filing standard, and the Agency **refuses** entry
where the statute contradicts the chapter (чл. 37, ал. 2). Get this answered before choosing the designation, not
after.

**(b) The charter says the root holds no personal data. Bulgarian law requires the entity to record people.**
The register publishes the устав and the founders; a сдружение must keep membership records and books of minutes
(чл. 40, ал. 1). These are not actually in conflict — the *root* holds no personal data about **agents or their
operators**, which is a statement about the trust system, while the *association* keeps records about **its own
members**, which is corporate housekeeping. But the устав must say so in terms, or a reader will find an apparent
contradiction between the charter published on the site and the statute filed at the registry.

## Everything that could NOT be verified

This list is part of the deliverable, not a caveat appended to it. Do not treat any of these as settled:

1. **The notarial tariff in euro.** The chamber's posted tariff prints лв. under a heading that says „(в лв.)" with
   an amendment history ending in 2009. No euro version was located. **Notarisation cost is unverified for 2026.**
2. **Apostille / legalisation for foreign founder documents.** The translation rules are verified (Наредба № 1
   чл. 6, ал. 3; ЗТРРЮЛНЦ чл. 18). The apostille layer above them is not — it was seen only in search summaries,
   which were not treated as sources.
3. **A material statutory conflict on the registrar's deadline.** ЗТРРЮЛНЦ чл. 19, ал. 2 gives **three working
   days**; ал. 3's faster next-working-day rule is written for **„търговци"** — yet the Registry Agency's own
   guidance applies ал. 3 to associations. On the literal text a сдружение falls under ал. 2. **This changes the
   timeline you would promise founders.**
4. Whether the public-benefit **activity report** rides the fee exemption in ЗТРРЮЛНЦ чл. 12, ал. 3 (which is
   keyed to Accounting Act documents) or attracts the чл. 16б announcement fee.
5. Whether the new automatic-publication route (чл. 19, ал. 8, in force 01.01.2026) reaches the activity report or
   only the financial statements.
6. The **А15 form itself** was never opened; the field descriptions here come from the AV instruction sheet.
7. Whether registrar *practice* demands notarised specimens despite ordinance and guidance both saying it does not.
8. **ЗМИП** beneficial-ownership filing cycle, deadlines and fees — not researched.
9. Sector-specific overlays: the AV warns that special laws add founder/member requirements for some purposes.
10. The full consolidated АВ fee tariff — only the euro extract for the relevant section was reachable; the
    justice ministry host failed TLS validation in this environment.
11. Whether the AV's чл. 52 citation error propagates into registrar practice or is a drafting slip.
