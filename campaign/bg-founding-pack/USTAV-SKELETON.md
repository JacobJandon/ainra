# DRAFT — FOR LICENSED REVIEW, NOT LEGAL ADVICE

# Устав skeleton — ЗЮЛНЦ чл. 20, clause by clause

**ЗЮЛНЦ чл. 20** lists thirteen things a statute *must* contain. This maps each to what AINRA's charter already
says, so the lawyer reviews one document rather than reconciling two.

> „Чл. 20. Учредителите приемат устав, който трябва да съдържа: 1. наименованието; 2. седалището; 3. целите и
> средствата за тяхното постигане; 4. определянето вида на дейност съгласно чл. 2; 5. предмета на допълнителната
> стопанска дейност; 6. органите на управление; 7. клоновете; 8. правомощията на органите на сдружението;
> 9. правилата относно начина на представляване на сдружението; 10. правилата относно възникването и
> прекратяването на членството, както и реда за уреждане на имуществените отношения при прекратяване на
> членството; 11. срока, за който е учредено сдружението; 12. реда за определяне на размера и начина на внасяне
> на имуществените вноски; 13. начина на разпределение на останалото имущество след удовлетворяването на
> кредиторите."
> — *lex.bg/laws/ldoc/2134942720, current through ДВ бр. 49 от 17 Юни 2025 г.*

## Clause 3 — целите (the four rules)

The charter's four rules are already written as obligations, which is what a statute needs. Suggested Bulgarian
skeleton, for the lawyer to correct:

> **Birth** — „Не се допуска издаване на идентичност без проверено и отговорно лице зад нея."
> **Life** — „Всяко удостоверение може да бъде проверено от всекиго, офлайн; проверката не се докладва на никого."
> **Death** — „Отнемане се извършва само от издателя, принципала или по надлежен ред; всяко отнемане е публично и
> подлежи на обжалване."
> **Neutrality** — „Коренът регистрира факти. Никога пари, лични данни, репутация или преценка."

**The средства (means) half of чл. 20, т. 3 is not optional** and is where a reviewer will look hardest: publishing
an open standard, operating a transparency log, accrediting independent registrars under one published rulebook,
and publishing revocation. Note that **чл. 37, ал. 2 lets the Agency refuse entry** where a public-benefit statute
contradicts the chapter, so vague aims are a filing risk, not a drafting preference.

## Clause 8 — правомощията (the six prohibitions)

The charter's prohibitions are the reason a neutral root can anchor rivals, so they belong where they bind: the
**powers** of the bodies. Drafted as limits, not aspirations — the association *may not*:

1. issue passports;
2. compute scores;
3. process payments;
4. hold personal data (about agents or their operators — see README (b));
5. gate L0 existence;
6. feature or favour any registrar.

**чл. 25, ал. 2 makes six ОС powers non-delegable** — „Правата по ал. 1, т. 1, 3, 7, 9, 11 и 12 не могат да се
възлагат на други органи" (statute amendment, election and removal of УС members, transformation or dissolution,
budget, УС activity report, annulment of other bodies' decisions). That is useful here: statute amendment cannot
be delegated away from the general meeting, which is the legal spine of "the charter is amendable only by
supermajority in every constituency after twelve months of public comment."

**Watch the interaction with чл. 29, ал. 2**: „Решения по чл. 25, ал. 1, т. 1 и 7 се вземат с мнозинство 2/3 от
присъстващите." The statute's default is two-thirds **of those present**. The charter promises a supermajority in
**every constituency** after a twelve-month comment period — a stricter rule. Bulgarian law generally permits a
statute to raise its own thresholds, but **whether it may condition an amendment on a twelve-month public comment
period is a question for the lawyer**, not an assumption to draft on.

## Clause 6 — органите, and the federation

**чл. 23**: „(1) Върховен орган на сдружението е общото събрание. (2) Управителен орган на сдружението е
управителният съвет." **чл. 30, ал. 1**: the УС „се състои най-малко от три лица - членове на сдружението", elected
for up to five years.

The charter's constituencies (issuers, attestors, verifiers, resolution and data providers, witnesses, public-
interest members) are not a legal form in the ЗЮЛНЦ. They must be expressed as **membership classes within the
общо събрание**, with the voting rules written into the устав.

**Custodians and witnesses stand structurally apart from the association** in the charter, and that should stay
true in the statute: they hold keys or cosign logs, and must not be members whose votes the board can influence.

**чл. 26, ал. 3** allows the устав to vary the one-month convocation notice; **чл. 27** allows it to vary quorum;
**чл. 28, ал. 3** allows it to vary the three-proxy limit. Each is a deliberate choice, not a default to inherit.

> ⚠️ **Online general meetings are not provided for by law.** The Registry Agency states it plainly: „Към
> настоящия момент ЗЮЛНЦ не предвижда изрична възможност ОС да заседава онлайн. Поради тази причина в устава
> следва да има изрична разпоредба." For a body whose members are spread across five-plus jurisdictions this is
> not a detail — **the устав must expressly permit remote participation in the ОС or the federation cannot meet.**
> (By contrast чл. 32, ал. 3 already allows remote УС participation, and ал. 5 allows written УС resolutions.)

## Clause 13 — residual assets, and forkability

If **обществена полза** is chosen, the destination of residual assets is tightly constrained: **чл. 43, ал. 2**
bars transfer to founders, current and former members, people who served on its bodies, employees, liquidators,
their spouses and relatives, and legal persons they control. **чл. 44** sends residual property, by court decision,
to a public-benefit NPO with the same or a similar purpose, and failing that to the municipality of the seat.

This sits *well* with the charter — the whole root is forkable from public data, and the assets that matter are
published, not owned. But the clause should be drafted deliberately so that the public record and the standard
survive dissolution, rather than leaving it to чл. 44's default.

## Two structural constraints to draft against

- **чл. 3, ал. 6** — „Юридическите лица с нестопанска цел не разпределят печалба." Aligns with *sells nothing*.
- **чл. 41, ал. 3–4** (public benefit only) — gratuitous disposals to related persons need a reasoned decision by
  **2/3 of all members** of the supreme body, and transactions with them are barred „освен ако сделките са в
  очевидна полза… или са сключени при общи условия, публично обявени." This is the legal analogue of *features no
  registrar, ever* — and it is stricter than the charter, so the charter does not need to be weakened to fit.
