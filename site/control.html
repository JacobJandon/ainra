      What this page cannot do.

      • A page served by a host that has been replaced can show anything, including green. The fingerprint above is for comparing out of band, and ainra ctl status from the signed release is the authority; this page is a convenience.
      • Directories do not expire. A host that keeps serving an old epoch cannot be told from there being no newer one until a ceremony cadence is published; this page says so rather than guess.
      • WITNESSES: 0 — SPLIT-VIEW GUARANTEE NOT IN FORCE · RECRUITING. With no independent witness, a log could show different histories to different readers, and nothing here would see it.
      • What it remembers (the newest epoch and each log's size) stays in this browser, and nothing is sent anywhere.
