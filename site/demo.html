What just happened

Three facts, checked with the root switched off.

01 · WHOTwo real signatures

Every passport is dual-signed — classical Ed25519 and post-quantum ML-DSA-65. Break either byte and it's alg_downgrade or sig_invalid.

02 · STILL TRUSTEDFresh revocation wins

Revocation lives in a status list with a freshness window. Replay an old "all-clear" snapshot from before a revocation and it fails stale_status — you can't roll the clock back to un-revoke.

03 · LOGGED FIRSTA Merkle proof to a checkpoint

The passport carries an inclusion proof your browser recomputes to a signed checkpoint root. Forge it and the root no longer matches — not_logged. No secret passports.
