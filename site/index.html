      Many issuers · one root
      
Everything orbits the root.

      
Registrars, passport services, ID schemes — each an island today, like domains before a common root. Chained to one neutral root they stay independent, and one check covers them all: trust earned anywhere is trust recognized everywhere.

    

    

      

        
      

      

        
THE ROOT — AINRA
Keys, rules, revocation, logs. Everything chains up to it; every check stays local and fast.

        
RING 1 · INDEPENDENT REGISTRARS
Separate organizations issuing under identical published rules.

        
RING 2 · AGENTS
Each carries a passport chained to the root; its running copies get short-lived keys.

        
RING 3 · VERIFIERS
Anyone deciding whether to trust an agent — the check runs on their own machine, and each picks its own bar.

        The problem
        
Anything can claim to be your agent.

        
Nobody can tell a real one from a fake.

      

      

      

        

        The fix
        
A passport it can prove.

        
Who it is, what it may do — signed. Checked in milliseconds; revoked in under a minute.

      

      

      

        

        The control
        
Yours, not ours.

        
You set the limits, you pull the switch — we just make them provable.

      

    

    
Tomorrow's checks look like today's — KYC for people, KYB for companies, KYA for agents: every acting AI carrying a scannable ID. The passport above already wears its machine-readable zone.

    
AINRA is a proposal for that neutral root — one open standard anyone can run, own, or fork. You are not asked to trust its founders; everything is public.

  






  
Status. Currently pre-ceremony: an operator-run genesis root exists; the recorded public ceremony across independent custodians is the pending milestone. Four implementations agree on all 745 CC0 conformance vectors; revocation is measured under 60 seconds in a local drill; verification is offline-capable, free, and phones home to no one. The passport above is a labeled specimen. By charter, enforced in code, the root issues no passports, computes no scores, holds no personal data, and sells nothing. Canonical live status: foundation.html.
